# Leave Policy

**Applies to:** All full-time employees
**Effective:** 1 January 2026
**Owner:** People Operations

---

## 1. Annual leave entitlement

Every full-time employee receives a fresh allocation at the start of each
calendar year:

| Leave type | Days per year |
|---|---|
| Annual leave | 18 |
| Casual leave | 8 |
| Sick leave | 8 |

Entitlement is granted in full on 1 January and is not accrued month by month.
An employee who joins partway through the year receives the full allocation for
that year.

Leave is counted in **working days only**. Saturdays and Sundays inside a leave
range are not deducted from your balance. A request from Friday to the following
Monday therefore costs two days, not four.

Your balance shows three numbers. **Allocated** is your entitlement for the
year. **Used** is leave already approved and taken. **Pending** is leave you
have requested but which has not yet been decided — those days are held against
your balance so they cannot be spent twice. **Available** is what remains:
allocated minus used minus pending.

---

## 2. Annual leave

Annual leave is for planned time off — holidays, family occasions, rest.

Annual leave should be requested **at least seven days before the first day
off**. This is not a system restriction; it is what your approver will expect,
and requests at shorter notice may be declined if they cannot be covered.
Requests during known busy periods should be discussed with your manager before
being submitted.

Unused annual leave does **not** carry over into the following year. Balances
reset on 1 January. Plan accordingly — leave taken in December must be
requested and approved before the year ends.

---

## 3. Casual leave

Casual leave covers short, unplanned absences that are not illness: a personal
errand, a family matter, a delayed journey home.

Casual leave is intended for **one or two days at a time**. A request for more
than three consecutive casual days should normally be annual leave instead, and
your approver may ask you to resubmit it as such.

Casual leave cannot be carried over.

---

## 4. Sick leave

Sick leave covers your own illness or injury.

You do not need to request sick leave in advance. Record it as soon as you are
able to — the same day if possible, and no later than your first day back.
Notify your manager directly as well; a leave request in the system is a record,
not a notification.

For an absence of **three or more consecutive working days**, People Operations
may ask for a medical certificate before approving. Keep any documentation your
doctor provides.

Sick leave is not a substitute for annual leave and should not be used for
planned medical appointments that could be scheduled outside working hours.
Routine appointments are casual leave.

---

## 5. How to request leave

You can request leave in two ways: through **Apply for Leave** in the portal, or
by asking the assistant in chat ("apply for two days casual leave next Monday
and Tuesday"). Both create the same request.

Every request needs a leave type, a start date, an end date, and a reason.

The following rules are enforced by the system and a request breaking any of
them will be rejected immediately:

- Leave **cannot start in the past**. Dates must be today or later.
- The end date cannot be **before** the start date.
- A single request **cannot span two calendar years**. Split leave that runs
  across 31 December into two requests.
- The range must contain **at least one working day**. A request covering only a
  Saturday and Sunday will be refused.
- A request **cannot overlap** another request of yours that is pending or
  approved. Cancel the existing one first if you are changing your plans.
- You must have **enough available days** of that type. If you have three casual
  days left, you cannot request four.

---

## 6. Approval

All leave requests are **pending until an approver decides them**. Submitting a
request is not the same as having it approved, and you should not make
non-refundable arrangements until it has been.

Requests are decided by People Operations or by an administrator. An approver
cannot decide their own request — someone else must review it, so that nobody
approves their own leave.

When a request is approved, the days move from pending to used. When it is
rejected, the days are returned to your available balance immediately.

An approver may add a note explaining a decision. If a request is declined, ask
your approver directly before resubmitting; the same request submitted again
without discussion will usually be declined again.

---

## 7. Cancelling leave

You can cancel your own request while it is still pending, or after it has been
approved if the leave has not yet started. Cancelling returns the days to your
available balance.

You cannot cancel someone else's request, and you cannot cancel leave that has
already been taken. If circumstances change during a period of approved leave,
speak to People Operations.

---

## 8. Public holidays

Public holidays are not deducted from your leave balance. The company observes
the public holidays published separately each year by People Operations.

If a public holiday falls inside a period of approved leave, it is still counted
as a working day by the system. Raise it with People Operations and the day will
be credited back to you manually.

---

## 9. Questions

For anything this policy does not cover — extended medical leave, unpaid leave,
bereavement, parental leave, or leave arrangements specific to your contract —
contact People Operations directly. The assistant can only answer from published
policy and from your own records, and will tell you when a question falls
outside what it can see.
