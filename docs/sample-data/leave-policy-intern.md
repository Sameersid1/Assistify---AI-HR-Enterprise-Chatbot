# Leave Policy — Interns

**Applies to:** Interns only
**Owner:** People Operations

Upload this with **Intern** ticked, so it is never read to anyone else.

---

## 1. Entitlement

Interns receive a reduced allocation reflecting the length of a typical
internship:

| Leave type | Days |
|---|---|
| Annual leave | 6 |
| Casual leave | 4 |
| Sick leave | 4 |

Leave is counted in **working days only**. Saturdays and Sundays inside a leave
range are not deducted, so Friday to the following Monday costs two days.

Entitlement does not increase if an internship is extended. If your internship
converts to a full-time role, your allocation is reset to the full-time
entitlement from the date of conversion, and leave already taken as an intern is
not carried across.

---

## 2. Taking leave as an intern

Because an internship is short, absence has a larger effect on it than the same
absence would on a permanent role. Two rules follow from that.

Annual leave should be requested **at least ten days in advance** — longer than
the seven days asked of permanent staff — so your supervisor can re-plan the
work you are responsible for.

Annual leave is not normally approved in the **first two weeks or the final two
weeks** of an internship. The first weeks are when you are being brought up to
speed, and the last are when your work is being handed over and assessed.

Casual leave is for short unplanned absences and is intended for one day at a
time. A request for more than two consecutive casual days should be annual leave
instead.

---

## 3. Sick leave

Sick leave covers your own illness. Record it as soon as you are able and notify
your supervisor directly — a request in the system is a record, not a
notification.

For three or more consecutive working days, People Operations may ask for a
medical certificate.

If illness causes you to miss more than five working days in total, your
supervisor and People Operations will discuss whether the internship dates
should be extended, so that you are assessed on a fair amount of work rather
than penalised for being unwell.

---

## 4. Examinations and academic commitments

Leave for university examinations is granted **in addition** to the entitlement
above and does not reduce your balance.

Request examination leave at least three weeks in advance and attach your
examination timetable. This is arranged directly with People Operations rather
than through the leave system, which has no separate examination category.

Academic commitments that are not examinations — project submissions, viva
preparation, coursework deadlines — are ordinary annual or casual leave.

---

## 5. Approval, cancellation, and the rules the system enforces

All requests are **pending until an approver decides them**. Submitting is not
approval, and you should not commit to travel before a request is approved.

You can cancel your own request while it is pending, or after approval if the
leave has not yet started. Cancelling returns the days to your balance.

The system will refuse a request that breaks any of these:

- Leave cannot start in the past.
- The end date cannot be before the start date.
- A request cannot span two calendar years.
- The range must contain at least one working day.
- A request cannot overlap another pending or approved request of yours.
- You must have enough available days of that type.

---

## 6. Unpaid leave and anything else

There is no unpaid leave arrangement for interns. If you need time away beyond
your entitlement, speak to People Operations and your supervisor — the usual
outcome is that the internship end date moves rather than that leave is granted.

For anything this policy does not cover, contact People Operations directly.
