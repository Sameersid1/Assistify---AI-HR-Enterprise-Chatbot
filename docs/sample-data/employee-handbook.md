# Employee Handbook — Everyday Procedures

**Applies to:** Everyone
**Owner:** People Operations

Upload this with **no employment type ticked**, so it applies to all staff.

---

## Lost or damaged ID card

Report a lost ID card on the day you notice it. An unreturned card is an access
risk, so the old card is deactivated before a new one is issued.

Email People Operations with your employee ID and where you last had the card.
Your access is revoked within the hour and a replacement is ready for collection
within two working days.

The first replacement in a twelve-month period is free. Further replacements
carry a ₹300 charge, deducted from the following month's salary. A card damaged
by ordinary wear is replaced free of charge — bring the damaged card with you.

If you lose your card while travelling for work, tell People Operations and do
not attempt to enter an office without an escort. Reception will issue a
temporary day pass against photo identification.

## Referral programme

Any employee may refer a candidate for an open role. Referrals are made through
People Operations with the candidate's CV and the role they are being referred
for.

A referral bonus is paid when the person you referred completes **six months**
of continuous service. The bonus is ₹25,000 for engineering roles and ₹15,000
for all other roles, paid with the following month's salary.

You may not refer an immediate family member, and you may not refer someone who
has already applied independently within the previous six months. If two
employees refer the same candidate, the earlier referral stands.

Referrals are not accepted for roles you would directly manage, to keep hiring
decisions and reporting lines separate.

## Expense claims

Claim work expenses within **30 days** of the spend. Claims older than that need
your manager's written approval before People Operations will process them.

Every claim needs an itemised receipt. A card statement is not a receipt — it
shows an amount but not what was bought.

Approved claims are reimbursed with the next payroll run. Claims submitted after
the 25th of a month usually land in the following month's payroll.

Travel booked through the company travel desk is billed directly and should not
be claimed. Claiming an expense the company has already paid is treated as a
conduct matter, not an administrative error.

## IT support and equipment

Raise IT problems with the IT Support team directly by email. Include what you
were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead — a screenshot saves a
round trip.

Company laptops are issued on joining and remain company property. Report loss
or theft the same day; a police report is required for insurance if the device
was stolen.

Software must be requested through IT Support rather than installed directly.
This is a licensing requirement, not a matter of trust.

Passwords are never requested by IT Support, People Operations, or anyone else,
by email or by phone. Any message asking for your password is fraudulent, and
should be forwarded to IT Support and then deleted.

## Working hours and remote work

Standard hours are 9:30 to 18:30, Monday to Friday, with an hour for lunch.

Hybrid staff are expected in the office at least two days a week, agreed with
their manager. Which days are chosen is a team decision, not an individual one —
the point is overlap, not attendance.

If you are working from home on a day you would normally be in, tell your
manager in advance. This is a courtesy, not an approval process.

---

## Grievances and conduct

Anything you cannot raise with your manager can be raised directly with People
Operations, in confidence.

Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation against someone who raises a concern
are treated as serious misconduct. Concerns are investigated by People
Operations and the outcome is communicated to the person who raised it.

Nothing in this handbook prevents you from raising a matter through any legal
route available to you.

---

## Address, bank, and personal details

Tell People Operations within a week of any change to your address, bank
account, or emergency contact.

Bank detail changes are confirmed by a phone call to the number already on your
record before the change takes effect. This delay is deliberate: payroll
redirection fraud almost always arrives as an email asking for an urgent change.

---

## What the assistant can and cannot answer

The assistant answers from documents published here and from your own records.
It cannot see payroll, performance reviews, or another employee's information.

For anything this handbook does not cover, contact People Operations. If the
assistant tells you a subject is not covered by published policy, that is
accurate — it is not guessing on your behalf.
